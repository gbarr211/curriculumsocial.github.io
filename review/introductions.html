Privacy, requests and blocked adults

Only the two adult accounts can open this request in the app. Your account email and private family or contribution records are not copied into it. Authorized infrastructure administrators may access stored records. Updates appear in the app; no introduction email is sent automatically.

Acceptance means the adult is open to an introduction. It does not book an event, join a pod, verify qualifications or agree supervision. Decide practical arrangements together. Events are independent from pods.

Hiding or deleting a shared card stops new requests to that card. Existing requests stay until either adult deletes them for both. Withdrawal saves the withdrawn status; blocking stops new requests. Remove saved introductions and your block list before deleting your sign-in account. Records have no automatic expiry; external account deletions currently need manual operator cleanup.
