Your family profile

Version: family-profile-2026-10-17. CurriculumSocial is operated by Gregory Barr. Contact: greg@curriculumsocial.com.

What is saved

Your family name or nickname, broad area, languages, educational philosophies, interests, introduction, optional picture, contribution and availability are linked to your adult account. We also save your visibility choice, notice version and creation/update times. No child records are needed.

Who can see it

Your profile starts private. If you select “Show our family in Find families,” signed-in members can see a card containing your family name, picture, area, languages, philosophies, interests and introduction. Your email, contribution and availability are not included in that card. Separately, you can invite an adult into your family using their verified sign-in email. After accepting, they can read your full saved family profile, including its picture, contribution and availability; the first parent keeps editing access. Other members may copy information they can see, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.

The project operator and authorized infrastructure administrators may access stored records. Google Firebase provides authentication and database infrastructure.

Family invitations

Family access saves adult display names, account identifiers, sharing consents and membership times. A waiting invitation saves the intended sign-in email, family and inviter names, expiry and status. Only the managing parent and verified intended recipient can open it. Its private link expires within 48 hours; expiration stops acceptance but does not automatically delete the record. Acceptance consumes the invitation record and saves the membership. The managing parent can revoke or remove waiting records and remove adult members; members can leave. Membership does not share private messages, contributions or Event meeting details, or establish guardianship.

Platform invitations use a signup link. Copying, device sharing or opening an email invitation uses your own app; CurriculumSocial does not send invitation emails, upload contacts or track referrals.

Pictures

A picture is optional. Choose an illustration or an adult photo you have permission to share. The browser creates a small square JPEG before upload and does not send the original file. Your picture is saved with your profile. It is visible on an enabled family card and to adults who accept your family invitations.

Change, export or delete

Edit your profile at any time. Turn off family visibility and save to remove your card from discovery. Download a copy or delete the profile and card from Your family. Before deleting your profile, close the shared family access you manage. Before deleting your sign-in account, leave other shared families and remove your saved records. Records stay until you delete them; no automatic expiry is currently implemented. Infrastructure logs and backups may follow provider retention.

Saving a profile does not enroll your family in a pod, approve teaching or hosting, or subscribe you to marketing. Leave out children’s names, exact addresses, health information and private contact details.
