Your family profile

Version: family-profile-2026-10-17. CurriculumSocial is operated by Gregory Barr. Contact: greg@curriculumsocial.com.

What is saved

Your family name or nickname, broad area, languages, educational philosophies, interests, introduction, optional picture, contribution and availability are linked to your adult account. We also save your visibility choice, notice version and creation/update times. No child records are needed.

Who can see it

Your profile starts private. If you select “Show our family in Find families,” signed-in members can see a card containing your family name, picture, area, languages, philosophies, interests and introduction. Your email, contribution and availability are not included in that card. Other members may copy information they can see, so share only what you are comfortable sharing.

The project operator and authorized infrastructure administrators may access stored records. Google Firebase provides authentication and database infrastructure.

Pictures

A picture is optional. Choose an illustration or an adult photo you have permission to share. The browser creates a small square JPEG before upload and does not send the original file. Your picture is saved with your profile and shared only if you enable your family card.

Change, export or delete

Edit your profile at any time. Turn off family visibility and save to remove your card from discovery. Download a copy or delete the profile and card from Your family. Delete your profile before deleting your sign-in account. Records stay until you delete them; no automatic expiry is currently implemented. Infrastructure logs and backups may follow provider retention.

Saving a profile does not enroll your family in a pod, approve teaching or hosting, or subscribe you to marketing. Leave out children’s names, exact addresses, health information and private contact details.
