Curriculum Social

Adult account notice

Updated 14 October 2026. CurriculumSocial is a project operated by Gregory Barr. This early release lets adults create a sign-in account and explore sample community workflows.

What is saved

Google Firebase Authentication processes your email, account identifier, sign-in provider and authentication metadata to operate and protect your account. Email/password accounts use Firebase-managed credentials. Google sign-in can also provide your name and profile image. You can optionally change or clear your own display name in account settings; this does not rename or publish your family profile. We do not request child records, addresses, payments or learning records in this release.

You can save a family profile and optionally share a family card with signed-in members. Your email stays private. Read the family profile privacy notice before saving. Delete your profile before deleting your sign-in account.

What is not submitted

Community examples are synthetic. Demo joins, votes, contributions and pod drafts do not enroll you, contact anyone or save a live interest submission. Creating an account is not enrollment or consent to marketing.

Control and retention

Your sign-in account remains until deleted. You can sign out or delete it from the account page; Firebase may require a fresh sign-in before deletion. Provider security logs may follow Google's retention policies. Do not enter sensitive or child information into demo fields.

Questions or account requests: greg@curriculumsocial.com. See Firebase privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Adult contributions

When contributions are activated, you can choose to share teaching, hosting or helping details privately with the community review team. A contribution includes your name, account email, description, availability and the review status and message. These details are not published to other families.

Updates appear in My contributions. Contribution submissions do not subscribe you to marketing or send contribution emails. Keep children's information and exact home addresses out of these forms.

You can update, withdraw or delete a contribution. Updating returns it for review. Withdrawal stops review but keeps its record; deletion removes the contribution and review update from the app. Delete your contributions before deleting your sign-in account. Administrative account deletions require separate record cleanup.

Acceptance is an agreement to follow up. It does not verify qualifications, confirm a booking or grant access to children's records.
