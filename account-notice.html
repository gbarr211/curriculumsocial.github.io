Curriculum Social

Adult account notice

Updated 21 October 2026. CurriculumSocial is operated by Gregory Barr. Adult accounts can save family profiles, contributions, introductions and free caregiver-attended Events. Demos are labeled separately.

Your account and family profile

Google Firebase Authentication processes your email, account identifier, sign-in provider and authentication metadata. Email/password accounts use Firebase-managed credentials. Google sign-in can provide your name and profile image. You can change or clear your account name without renaming your family profile.

Your family profile starts private. You can choose to share a family card with signed-in members; your email stays private. Read the family profile privacy notice for the saved fields and visibility choices.

Contributions

Teaching, hosting and helping contributions include your name, account email, description, availability and review updates. The full submission is private to you and the authorized review team. If you opt in, an approved card shares only your selected display name, introduction, broad area, interests and availability with signed-in members. Other members may copy information they can see.

You can update, withdraw or delete a contribution. Updates return it for review and hide its card until approved again. Withdrawal hides the card but keeps the record until you delete it. Acceptance starts follow-up; it does not verify qualifications or confirm arrangements.

Introductions

A request saves your display name, message, selected family or contribution card, recipient, replies and status. It is shared with the named adult managing that card; your account email is not shared. Either participant can delete the shared request. Blocking prevents further introduction requests from that adult.

Events

Hosts save their name, gathering description, schedule, broad area, capacity and meeting details. Signed-in members can browse listings. A household request saves the requesting adult’s chosen household name, numbers of attending adults and children, caregiver confirmation, host replies and status. No child names or student profiles are needed.

Exact meeting details are separate from the listing and available only to the host and currently approved requesting adults before the gathering ends. Cancellation removes those details. Each requesting adult manages their own household request; approval does not grant access to other family accounts.

Updates and demos

Contribution, introduction and Event updates appear in the app; these workflows do not send notification emails. Account verification and password-reset emails are separate. Demos use fictional people and do not send requests or create real bookings or child records. Creating an account does not enroll you in a pod or subscribe you to marketing.

Control and retention

Remove your saved family profile, contributions, shared introductions, hosted Events and household requests before deleting your sign-in account. The app checks for remaining records; Firebase may require a fresh sign-in. Deletions performed outside the app need separate operator cleanup. Records have no automatic expiry; provider logs and backups may follow Google’s retention policies.

Keep children’s information and sensitive details out of profiles and contributions. Hosts should put exact meeting details only in the Event’s private location fields.
