Hosting, privacy and your saved plans

Every household brings a caregiver who stays and supervises its children. A host organizes the gathering; hosting does not make them responsible for supervising everyone’s children. Account email confirmation is not a safety, identity, home or credential check.

Your household display name, adult and child counts, confirmation and request status are visible only to you and the host. Other attendees do not receive a guest list. Exact meeting details are stored separately and shared in the app only with the host and approved attendees. Keep addresses, contact details and children’s identities out of the listing and display names.

Approval reserves guest places. Pending requests do not. Changing an approved request releases its places and sends it back for approval. Leaving or cancellation removes location access in the app; previously viewed details cannot be taken back.

Updates appear here. No automatic emails, reminders, refunds or payments are involved. Check your saved plan before leaving home. To change the time or meeting place, the host cancels the gathering and creates a new one.

You can delete your inactive RSVP. Hosts cancel before deleting a listing and its location; attendees can still remove their own remaining RSVP history. There is no automatic expiry. Cancel and remove your hosted listings and RSVP records before deleting your account. Account deletions outside the app require manual operator cleanup.
